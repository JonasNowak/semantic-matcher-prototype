# POL-MD-03: Workplace Surveillance & Employee Monitoring

**Framework**: European Works Council Standards
**Fields**: SURVEILLANCE, HR_EMPLOYMENT
**Keywords**: keystroke: 3.0, tastaturanschlag: 3.0, mitarbeiterüberwachung: 3.0, webcam monitor: 2.5, productivity score: 2.2

Restricts systems designed for continuous tracking, keystroke logging, employee screen recording, webcam monitoring, or automated behavioral and productivity scoring of workforce members.
